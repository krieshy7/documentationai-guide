---
title: App Not Responding
description: Fix issues when PerceptEye AI won't load or respond.
---

## Symptoms

- PerceptEye AI dashboard freezes
- No new responses from the AI agent

## Quick Fix

<Steps>
  <Step title="Refresh the page">
    Try refreshing your browser tab or clearing cache.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check server status">
    Visit `https://status.percepteye.ai` for outages.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Re-login">
    Logout and login again at `https://dashboard.percepteye.ai`.
  </Step>
</Steps>
