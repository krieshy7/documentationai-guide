## Overview

Set up a PerceptEye AI autonomous agent for your project.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create new agent">
    Navigate to **Agents** in the dashboard. Click **New Agent**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Configure agent">
    Name your agent, select capabilities, and set your environment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Deploy">
    Click **Deploy**. Your agent is now live and learning!
  </Step>
</Steps>
